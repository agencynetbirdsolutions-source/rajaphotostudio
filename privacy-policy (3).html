Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Raja Photo Studio ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you visit our website or contact us on WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We may collect: (a) information you choose to share when you message us on WhatsApp, such as your name, phone number and details of the service you need; (b) photographs or files you voluntarily send us for printing or editing; and (c) basic technical information collected automatically, such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate location, through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiries, provide the services you request, improve our website and services, measure the performance of our advertising, and meet legal obligations.

3. Your Photographs

Photographs or files you send us are used only to deliver the service you asked for, such as printing or editing. We do not publish, sell or share your photographs, including on social media or in our portfolio, without your permission.

4. Cookies and Advertising

We may use cookies and similar technologies, including those from Google (such as Google Ads and Google Analytics), to understand how visitors use our site and to show relevant ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or by changing your browser settings. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. WhatsApp Communication

When you tap a WhatsApp button, you are taken to WhatsApp, a third-party service governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Messages you send are used only to respond to you and deliver your service. We will stop messaging you if you ask us to.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example, analytics and advertising platforms) or when required by law.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

8. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information or photographs, or object to their use, by messaging us on WhatsApp.

9. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through it. Photographs of minors should be shared only by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

11. Contact Us
